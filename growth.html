Growth

The project I think best deomstrates my growth in terms of computer science is my final project from fall semester. For this project, I created a recommendation app on App Lab that asked the user to pick between 4 drink options under 3 different categories; sodas, fun drinks and hot drinks. Based on the users choices, the app recommended a Disney sidekick! I think this project best shows my growth because I had to incorporate all of the things that I learned first semester into one overall project.
