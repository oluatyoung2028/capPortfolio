Best Work

I can condifently say that my best work was my unit 3 interactive game. For this game, the user can control a princess using the left, right, up, and down arrow keys. The objective of the game is to touch the unicorn and avoid the dragon that are moving across the screen. Interacting with the unicorn increases your score by 5 and touching the dragon decreases your health by 3. Reach a score of 100 and you win but if your health gets to 0 the game restarts! I consider this my best work because it I had to do a lot of troubleshooting and I put a lot of work into making sure that the game worked the way I wanted it to.
